Model Overview

Deterministic compartmental system dynamics model (Stella Architect v1.9.5, Euler integration, Δt=1 month, T=120 months). Four HIV care cascade stocks: Undiagnosed, Diagnosed Not in Care, In Care Not Suppressed, Virally Suppressed. SUD enters through three pathways: transmission amplification, care instability, and overdose mortality.

Initial Conditions — Missouri 2022 (CDC AtlasPlus)

  • Total modeled PLHIV: 15,322
  • Undiagnosed: 2,145 (~14% of total)
  • Diagnosed, not in care: 3,057
  • In care, not suppressed: 1,542
  • Virally suppressed: 8,578 (65.1% of diagnosed)

NLS Calibration

β, dx_rate, linkage_rate fitted to CDC AtlasPlus MO 2013–2022 (n=9, excluding 2020) using L-BFGS-B NLS. 95% CIs via parametric bootstrap (500 resamplings).

  • RMSE (diagnoses): 25/year (mean bias −8)
  • RMSE (suppression): 1.7 pp
